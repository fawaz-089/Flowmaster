STEP 1 — HOW MUCH DEMULSIFIER PER HOUR?

                        
Demulsifier L/H = (PPM × Total Flow) ÷ 1,000

                        

                            = (0 × 0) ÷ 1,000
                        

                        
= 0.0 L/H total

                        

                            Why ÷ 1,000? Total Flow is in m³/hr but Demulsifier is in L/hr. Since 1 m³ = 1,000 L, dividing by 1,000 keeps the units consistent with PPM (parts per million).

📊 BPD

                
BPD = m³/h × 151

                
Converts hourly flow rate (m³/h) to Barrels Per Day.

            

            

                
📈 MBD

                
MBD = (Total × 151) ÷ 1000

                
Converts total flow to Thousand Barrels per Day — used for reporting plant throughput.

            

            

                
⚙️ Pump Flow

                
Flow (L/H) = 3600 ÷ Seconds

                
Measures pump output in Liters per Hour from one-liter stroke time.

            

            

                
📉 Performance

                
Perf % = (Actual ÷ Expected) × 100

                
Expected = 54 × (Stroke% / 100)

                
Compares the pump's actual flow against its expected design flow at the same stroke setting.

            

            

                
⚠️ Error

                
Error % = Performance % - 100

                
+ = Over-performing | - = Under-performing

                
Shows how far the pump deviates from its rated performance — guides maintenance action.

            

            

                
🧪 Convert L/H To GPD

                
GPD = (L/H ÷ 3.7854) × 24

                
Converts pump flow to US Gallons per Day. The constant 3.7854 = 1 US gallon in liters.

            

            

                
🎯 PPM

                
PPM = (Demulsifier Flow L/hr × 1,000) ÷ Total Flow m³/hr
