📊 BPD

BPD = m³/h × 151
Converts hourly flow rate (m³/h) to Barrels Per Day.

📈 MBD

MBD = (Total × 151) ÷ 1000
Converts total flow to Thousand Barrels per Day — used for reporting plant throughput.

⚙️ Pump Flow

Flow (L/H) = 3600 ÷ Seconds
Measures pump output in Liters per Hour from one-liter stroke time.

📉 Performance

Perf % = (Actual ÷ Expected) × 100
Expected = 54 × (Stroke% / 100)
Compares the pump's actual flow against its expected design flow at the same stroke setting.

⚠️ Error

Error % = Performance % - 100
+ = Over-performing | - = Under-performing
Shows how far the pump deviates from its rated performance — guides maintenance action.

🧪 GPD (Liters per Hour → US Gallon per Day)

GPD = (L/H ÷ 3.7854) × 24
Converts pump flow to US Gallons per Day. The constant 3.7854 = 1 US gallon in liters.

🎯 PPM

PPM = (GPD ÷ (MBD × 42)) × 1000
Calculates demulsifier concentration in crude oil. 42 = US gallons per barrel.

How to use the triangle

Simple rule: Cover the variable you want to find — the remaining layout shows the formula.

  • 🟢 Need PPM? Cover it → you get: (GPD × 1000) ÷ (MBD × 42)
  • 🟡 Need GPD? Cover it → you get: PPM × MBD × 42 ÷ 1000
  • 🔵 Need MBD? Cover it → you get: (GPD × 1000) ÷ (PPM × 42)

The idea: In this triangle layout, anything on top equals the two items below it multiplied together, and anything on the bottom equals the top divided by the other bottom item.

🤔 Where does the × 1000 come from?
It's hiding inside the names of our units. Let's look inside MBD and PPM to find out.

= 832 ÷ 15,124,200

PPM
= (832 ÷ (360.1 × 42)) × 1,000

Same answer. Both correct.

The one-line insight:
The ×1000 in the formula is just the difference between "thousand" (hidden in MBD) and "million" (hidden in PPM).

1,000,000 ÷ 1,000 = 1,000
